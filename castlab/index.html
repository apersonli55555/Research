CastCheck shoreline twin

A terrain model of a saved location built from public elevation data, with a water level you can raise or lower to see where the shoreline moves, how steep the bank is, and how the simulated shoreline compares with GPS points you record in the field.

Water level

Display

Bank model below the waterline

Elevation models record the water surface, not the lake bed. For drawdowns the bed is extrapolated by continuing the measured bank slope under water, capped at this depth. Lowered-water results are therefore an assumption, labelled in the readout.

Design search

Every combination in the grid is solved at the design load. Designs below the minimum safety factor are rejected; the rest are scored and the non-dominated (Pareto) set is marked.

Static tip-load bench test

  1. Clamp the blank horizontally at the point where the simulated length starts (top of the grip).
  2. Measure the unloaded tip height against a vertical rule.
  3. Hang known masses from the tip-top (weigh them on a kitchen scale) and record the tip height each time.
  4. Enter drop = unloaded height − loaded height. Repeat each mass at least twice.
