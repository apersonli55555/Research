What counts as a fall

A fall is when a person unintentionally comes to rest on the ground, floor or another lower level (the WHO definition).

  • Fall: a person trips, slips, collapses or tumbles and ends up on the ground, floor, a lower step, into a pool, and so on.
  • Not a fall: other accidents (something breaks, a ball hits someone, an animal falls), a person who stumbles but stays upright, or someone who sits or lies down on purpose.
  • Can't tell: the person is off-screen, the view is too blurry, or you honestly can't decide. These clips are left out of the results.
